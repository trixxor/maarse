Decisions

What was decided, what was rejected, and the numbers behind it.

TL;DR

What: The decision log records the choices that shaped Må:rse, including the ideas that were tried or parked, with the numbers behind them. Nothing is deleted: a decision that is replaced is marked as superseded.

Why: A design is only trustworthy if you can see what was not done and why. A rejected idea with numbers stops the same idea being proposed again without new evidence.

Strengths

  • Every decision has context, options, decision, consequences and evidence
  • Rejected ideas keep their measurements
  • Parked ideas say how to resume

Good to know

  • Some decisions rest on simulation only
  • A few are decided by the project owner without a measurement, for example one radio setting for everybody

Why some ideas were rejected

Load control and path request quotas rejected (measured)

A node over 25 % channel load would send at most 6 path requests a minute and double its flood delay. It helped under stress at SF11 (200 messages: 64 to 75 % delivered), but not at SF12 (43 to 39 %), and at normal load it cost deliveries (SF11, 120 nodes: 90 to 85 %) for 10 to 20 % less airtime. A 40 % threshold was no better. The project prefers delivery and stability over airtime savings, so the firmware does not use it.

Announce policies rejected (measured)

Jitter, 70 % forwarding and scope 2 moved airtime by −7 to +11 % in both directions with no pattern, and 70 % forwarding cost 3 points in the forest. Removed again.

A separate faster channel for trunks not chosen

A faster second band would give more capacity, but then not every device could hear every other. Decision 0003 keeps one setting and spends the shared channel better instead.

Firmware updates over LoRa shelved

The OTA code over LoRa is kept in the repository but not pursued. It is not to be proposed with compression, delta updates or hardware tests unless the decision is reopened.
