Android: getting around, and out of the browser

Three ways to fix it. All three keep one rule: the four places (Chats, Nodes, Network, Settings) are always one tap away, and Back always means the same thing.

Today: a chat, a contact, Pages and every sub-page hide the bottom bar and pile up on a stack. To get back to Chats from a page you have to press Back through every page you visited, then through the start page, then out of Nodes. There is no direct way out.

A · The bar never hides recommended

The bottom bar stays on every screen, also in a chat and in the browser. Pages becomes a place of its own in the bar, so the browser is never "inside" something. One tap on any place takes you there; the browser remembers where you were. The browser's own buttons stay at the top.

C · Browser toolbar at the bottom, app menu one swipe away

Like phone browsers today: the browser's buttons sit at the bottom where the thumb is. The app's four places are in a sheet that a tap on ☰ (or a swipe up on the toolbar) opens. More room for the page than A, and still two taps to Chats.

Back, the same everywhere

Back (the phone's ◁ or the ← at the top-left): one step back where you are: the previous page in the browser, the chat list from a chat. When there is nothing more to go back to in a place, it goes to Chats (the home place), and from Chats it leaves the app. It never walks through the start page or through pages you left long ago.
A tap on the place you are already in (e.g. Pages while reading a page) takes you to the top of that place: the browser's start page, the chat list, the node list.
PC web client: the same places in the left rail (already always visible); Back in the browser is the page's ←, and the browser's history lives in the page area only.
